BOQ Software by Industry

Quantara supports contractors, estimators, and consultants across multiple sectors by providing structured BOQ and document workflows.

Contractors

Organize tender documents, subcontractor packages, and project revisions.

Verified capabilities for standard project records.

Quantity Surveyors

Manage BOQ preparation, tender documentation, and rigorous revision control.

Assists workflows; does not replace regulated QS practice.

MEP Contractors

Structure multi-discipline BOQs, coordinate specs, and manage schedules.

No automated drawing measurement currently active.

HVAC Contractors

Organize equipment, ductwork, and piping schedules efficiently.

No automated duct or pipe takeoff active.

Fit-Out Companies

Manage detailed interior BOQs, frequent client variations, and proposals.

No automatic room detection available.

Fire-Fighting Contractors

Organize technical BOQs, system components, and equipment schedules.

Does not validate engineering compliance.

Facilities Management

Structure BOQs for refurbishment projects and complex service packages.

Not an asset-management or CMMS platform.

Engineering Consultants

Manage technical schedules, document revisions, and controlled templates.

Does not provide formal design validation.
